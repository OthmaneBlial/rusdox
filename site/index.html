Where the wedge matters

Keep editability, direct PDF output, and automated review in one system.

General converters, DOCX libraries, and PDF typesetters are excellent at different jobs. RusDox is deliberately narrower: dual-output business-document automation with proof that both artifacts contain the supported source semantics.

Structured specs

Developer-owned reports with a versioned schema.

Use readable YAML, JSON, or TOML for board packs, invoices, dashboards, and recurring exports. Validation points back to the source before rendering starts.

Word-native templates

Approved designs stay editable after automation.

Populate bounded placeholders, loops, and conditions in a designer-authored DOCX while preserving untouched package parts byte for byte.

Reviewable evidence

Make document parity visible in local work and CI.

Reopen DOCX, inspect PDF structure, compare semantic order, retain page snapshots, and add an optional visual baseline without sending content to a diff service.

Choose structured data or a Word template

Specs suit developer-owned layouts. Word templates suit approved designs that must remain familiar and editable to non-developers.

02

Render both artifacts from one typed model

DOCX and PDF writers share validated content, metadata, page controls, and the documented layout subset. There is no hidden Office conversion step.
